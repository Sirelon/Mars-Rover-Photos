See Mars as the rovers see it. Mars Rover Photos brings you thousands of real images captured on the surface of the Red Planet by NASA's robotic explorers, straight from the official NASA archives.

Browse photos from every mission in the app and dig into the science behind them, whether you're a space enthusiast, a student, or just curious about what another world looks like.

FEATURES

• Eight missions, one app — Explore images from Perseverance, Curiosity, the Ingenuity helicopter, Spirit, Opportunity, InSight, and the Viking 1 and 2 landers.

• The first photo from Mars — Browse 2,914 frames from the Viking archive, including the first photograph ever taken on the Martian surface.

• Real NASA photography — Every image comes directly from NASA's Mars mission archives.

• Popular photos — See the most-viewed and most-loved shots from the community.

• Favorites — Save the images you love and revisit them anytime, even offline.

• Full-screen gallery — Pinch to zoom and study Martian landscapes in stunning detail.

• Save & share — Keep photos in your library or share the wonder of Mars with friends.

• Mission info & facts — Learn about each mission and fascinating discoveries from the Red Planet.

• Jump by date — Travel to any sol (Martian day) and see what the rovers captured.

Download Mars Rover Photos and start your journey across Mars today.
